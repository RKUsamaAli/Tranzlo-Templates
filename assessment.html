Developmental milestones
Milestones now interpret themselves

What changed — Each milestone is a number in months, plus a "Not achieved yet" option, plus an automatic flag comparing the entry against normal ranges: Within range / Mild delay / Significant delay.

Why — Two reasons. First, a child with cerebral palsy who has never walked cannot be given a number, so the original form has no valid answer for the most important cases. Second, "walking 35 months" only means something to a reader who knows that typical is 12 — the form should say so, not rely on memory. Type a value below and watch the flag.

5 Systems Review

From your image 4
"Radio Selection" changed to multi-select — this one matters

What changed — Your form marks the Respiratory row as a radio button, meaning only one option can be chosen. We have made it multi-select, with Clear behaving as an exclusive option: tick Clear and everything else clears; tick any finding and Clear clears itself. The same rule now applies to Skin (Intact), Vision/Hearing (Normal) and Birth Complications (Nil).

Why — A child can have a tracheostomy and secretions and be on oxygen at the same time. A radio button forces the therapist to record only one of the three, and the two they drop are usually the two that matter. Try it below — tick two findings, then tick "Clear".

Neurological split into two questions

What changed — Your single Neurological row mixed Alert / GCS impaired (level of consciousness) with Hypotonia / Hypertonia / Spasticity / Ataxia (motor findings). We have separated them into two questions.

Why — They are different clinical axes and a child has an answer to both at once. Mixing them in one list means the therapist must choose between recording that the child is alert and recording that the child is spastic.

10 Posture & Muscle Tone

From your image 5 — table rebuilt
The Ashworth table has been rebuilt — please read this one

What we found — On your form the table header reads Body Region | Left MAS Grade | Right MAS Grade | Notes, but the rows pair two different muscles: "Elbow Flexors" in column 1 and "Elbow Extensors" in column 3. So the column labelled "Right MAS Grade" is not the right side of the body — it is the grade of a second, different muscle. As drawn, the table cannot record left-versus-right.

What changed — Each muscle group is now one row, with a genuine Left and Right grade. You select which groups you want from a catalogue, exactly as in sections 11 and 12.

Why — In cerebral palsy and hemiplegia the difference between the two sides is the finding. A form that cannot express it cannot support the treatment decision or show progress.

11 Range of Motion

From your image 6
ROM is recorded in degrees, not as a score out of 4 — decision needed

What we found — Your form records range of motion as 1/4, i.e. a score out of four. Range of motion is an angular measurement taken with a goniometer; there is no recognised 0–4 range-of-motion scale.

Why it matters — A child whose hip abduction improves from 20° to 45° has made real, reportable progress, but on a 0–4 scale they stay at "2/4" and the improvement is invisible. You also lose comparison against normal ranges and the ability to produce outcome reports for referrers or funders.

What we built — Both. The prototype ships in degrees, with the normal range printed under each motion and out-of-range values highlighted. Your original 0–4 scale is a one-line switch in the configuration file (romMode: 'graded') if you decide to keep it. We need your decision on this before implementation.

"Shoulder Flex/Ext" split into separate motions

What changed — Rows that combined two opposite motions (Shoulder Flex/Ext, Hip Flex/Ext, Ankle DF/PF) are now separate rows.

Why — Flexion and extension have completely different normal ranges, and a child commonly has full flexion with a severe extension limitation. One value for both hides exactly the finding you are looking for. The list is longer, but because you choose what to load, you never see a motion you did not test.

Every measurement can be marked "Not Tested" with a reason

What changed — You asked that all fields be required. We have kept that, but added an NT button on every measurement cell. A cell is complete when it holds either a value or Not-Tested plus a reason. Blank is still an error.

Why — Some measurements genuinely cannot be taken: the child would not cooperate, a contracture blocks the movement, the limb is in a cast, the side is unaffected. If the form refuses to accept that, therapists type a "1" to get past validation — and then your data says the child has severe restriction when they were simply not tested. An explicit "not tested, because…" is honest data; a forced number is not.
