What is in this prototype

Sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 are built from the images you supplied — including the reflex screen, the WHO gross motor milestones and the fine motor domains. Sections 1, 6, 7 and 9 were referenced by your numbering but no image was provided, so we have drafted reasoned versions marked DRAFT. Everything from section 15 onward is a labelled placeholder.

The assessment is one scrolling page, matching your existing OPD layout — every card is open, there are no Back / Next buttons, and Toggle Sections at the top right hides any card you do not use or jumps you straight to one.

We changed a number of things. Change notes are hidden by default — press Show change notes at the top of the assessment and every change is explained in a purple CHANGE panel on the card it applies to. A consolidated list is on the What we changed & why page.

One decision is needed before implementation: range of motion is recorded as a score out of 4 on your form. We have built it in degrees, with your 0–4 scale available as a configuration switch. See section 11, or the change notes page.
