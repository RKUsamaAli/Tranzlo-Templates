What is in this prototype

Sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 11 and 12 are built from the images you supplied. Sections 1 and 6–9 were referenced by your numbering but no image was provided, so we have drafted reasoned versions marked DRAFT — please confirm or replace them. Everything from section 13 onward is a labelled placeholder.

We changed a number of things. Each change is explained on the screen where it appears, in a purple CHANGE panel you can expand or hide with the "Show change notes" switch at the top of the assessment. A consolidated list is on the What we changed & why page.

One decision is needed from you before implementation: range of motion is currently recorded as a score out of 4 on your form. We have built it in degrees, with your 0–4 scale available as a configuration switch. See section 11, or the change notes page.
